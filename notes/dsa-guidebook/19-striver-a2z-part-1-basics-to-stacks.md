# Striver A2Z part 1 (basics to stacks)

Every item of the Striver A2Z DSA sheet in its official order, with the difficulty from takeUforward. The Hint column tells you which guidebook chapter has step-by-step hints for that question. In the LeetCode column: a number is the LeetCode question number, "yes" means it is on LeetCode (search the name there), and "-" means it is on takeUforward / GeeksforGeeks instead (search the name on takeuforward.org). Striver sometimes rates a question differently from LeetCode; the difficulty shown here is the one from the Striver sheet.
